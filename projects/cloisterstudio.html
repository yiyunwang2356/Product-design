Cloister Studio

Freedom to Move and See — Connecting user behavior to space to create an open, bright, and user-friendly living-working workflow.

Role

Project Lead & Lead Designer

Date / Location

2025 Taipei

Collaborators

Shan Shan Chuan Design Studio (Construction)

Freedom to Move and See

Hybrid Co-Existence

My goal was to make an open space where living and working can happen together. I used angled walls and diagonal cuts to divide the rooms without using heavy traditional walls.

Continuous Ceiling Plane

To make the small layout feel unified, I detached the TV cabinet from the ceiling. By keeping the ceiling plane continuous, I prevented a visual break. This allows the space to feel more expansive and open.

Deep Daylight Penetration

Working from home shouldn't feel like being in a box. I designed a layout that brings in natural light from the windows to the deepest parts of the floor plan. Using transparent and perforated materials, the studio stays bright and open, helping the client stay energized and focused all day.

Architectural Diagram & Flow Analysis

Natural Light Ingress

Bring in the natural light: The upper geometry redirects external daylight past glass partitions, illuminating both the workspace and interior private quarters.

Circular Circulation

Easy Flow: You can walk through the space in a smooth, circular way. The absence of traditional dead-end hallways creates fluid movement between working and living routines.

Spatial Experience & Flow

1. Entry Threshold

Detached the storage from the ceiling, automatically dividing different functional areas without visual barriers.

2. Co-Working Hub

Flexible Walls: Used metal and glass sliding doors. Close them for a quiet, private office atmosphere.

3. Glazed Partition

Open them completely to make the whole house feel like one single, continuous, and spacious room.

4. Private Quarters

Master room with sliding door integration, seamlessly connecting the suite, working area, and dressing room.

Material Palette & Tactile Contrast

Warmth & Structure

Paired warm natural wood with concrete grey tones to create a rich, tactile contrast between soft organic comfort and modern structural clarity.

Dynamic Storage & Living Wall

Developed perforated metal doors as a flexible storage system. They serve as hanging space while doubling as a living wall for plants, bringing nature into the modern interior.

User Behavior Integration

By connecting user behavior directly to the spatial layout, the design supports a dynamic, adaptable lifestyle with an effortless living-working workflow.

Design Execution & Workflow Optimization

1. Angled Geometry & Circulation

Mapped circular paths to eliminate traditional corridors, increasing usable living area while enabling a smooth, user-friendly movement flow.

2. Ceiling Continuation & Joinery

Engineered detached floating cabinetry with Shan Shan Chuan Design Studio to preserve uninterrupted ceiling sightlines across zones.

3. Perforated Material Light Diffusion

Positioned metal perforations and transparent glass doors so daylight penetrates from exterior windows deep into interior working zones.

4. Integrated Biophilic Storage

Crafted modular metal doors that double as functional hanging systems and vertical indoor plant displays for everyday well-being.
