BookingPie

A booking system for small service businesses, redesigned to reduce friction from manual LINE-based reservation workflows and create a clearer digital service experience.

Role

Service Flow Analyst / UX Lead

Focus

Service Design / Flow Optimization / Product Thinking

Challenge

Manual booking communication was fragmented, time-consuming, and easy to lose.

Project gallery

Process

1. Understand the booking journey

Mapped the current flow from inquiry to confirmation to identify drop-off points and operational friction.

2. Restructure the service flow

Created clearer states, user decisions, and booking logic to reduce confusion.

3. Translate into product UI

Designed key screens and product interactions around booking clarity and trust.

4. Show the product outcome

Used concise storytelling to explain how service design improved the digital product.
