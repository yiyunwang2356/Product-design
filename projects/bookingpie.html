Booking Management Platform

BookingPie

BookingPie is a booking management platform designed to simplify reservation management and reduce manual coordination for small service businesses.

Timeline

2025 · Taipei

Role

Service Flow Analysis · UX Planning

Team

Worked with a PM and Workflow Automation Specialist

Status

Deployed at Nomadd Haus · Testing with Sparkle Bar

User journey

Inquiry: Customer sends a LINE message: "Is Saturday free?"

Lookup: Merchant checks a paper notebook or personal calendar.

Response: Merchant replies: "Only 2 PM is available."

Delay: Customer replies 30 minutes later: "Okay, I'll take it."

Risk: The time slot may already be taken, or the booking may not be recorded.

Field Research & Insights

Fragmented User Flow

Customers switch between the booking page and LINE to check availability and confirm their booking.

Manual Work

Business owners spend time replying to messages and confirming reservations one by one.

Lost Bookings

Customers may drop off while waiting for a reply, especially when the process takes too long.

Design Solution

One Booking Flow

Customers can check availability and complete a booking in one place.

Centralized Information

Reservations and availability are kept in one place.

Automatic Confirmation

Bookings are confirmed automatically without back-and-forth messages.

Calendar Integration

Bookings are synced with the business calendar.

The "To-Be" Flow

From a manual process to a more structured system.

Customer: Selects an available time slot on BookingPie.

System: Checks availability and confirms the booking.

Automation: Creates a calendar event and sends an email confirmation.

Reminder: Automatically sends a reminder to the customer through LINE.

Merchant: Views all bookings in one synchronized dashboard.

Design Process

1. Understand the booking journey

2. Restructure the flow

Clarified user decisions and booking logic.

3. Design the product UI

Turned the booking flow into clear screens and interactions.

4. Test and refine

Reviewed the product flow and refined the experience based on feedback.
