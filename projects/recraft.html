Concept Project

Re:Craft

A future-facing concept system exploring circular design, product vision, and speculative service possibilities.

Role

System Designer

Focus

Vision / Exploration

Challenge

How might a more circular product and service system be structured in a meaningful and usable way?

Project gallery

Process

1. Frame the design question

Started with a broader systems question instead of a fixed product brief.

2. Explore circular logic

Mapped scenarios, product relationships, and possible service loops.

3. Shape a concept system

Turned the exploration into a coherent concept with visible logic and touchpoints.

4. Present the vision

Used the case to show perspective, range, and future-facing design thinking.
