Circular Economy & Product Design

Circular Furniture Design

I led the development of a design ecosystem that transforms high-quality construction remnants into bespoke, adhesive-free furniture.

Role

Design Lead / Circular Economy / Product & UI

Date / Location

2025–Current, Taipei

Challenge

Diverting urban construction remnants from landfills into modular, customizable furniture backed by a seamless digital ordering interface.

The Reality of Construction Waste

The Insight

While working in Interior Design, I noticed that huge amounts of high-quality marble and wood are thrown away every day.

The Problem

These materials take a lot of energy and carbon to produce. However, they are often discarded simply because they are the "wrong size" for standard building projects.

The Goal

My goal is to give these valuable materials a second life. I am designing a digital marketplace where they can be saved and reused. This system connects construction waste with designers who can turn small pieces into new products.

The Digital Platform

Material Traceability

Data-driven insights on origin and carbon footprint.

Commission Inquiry

Direct channel for architects and designers to source and customize.

Parametric UI Configurator

Empowers clients to design bespoke spatial furniture pieces tailored to exact architectural dimensions in real time.

How it happens

Step 1: material provided

Customers provide high-quality materials left over from their own projects.

Step 2: Re:craft platform

The digital platform handles the request and tracks the material's details.

Step 3: design & craft

We design a custom piece that fits the exact size and shape of the customer's material.

Step 4: new life

We return the material as a bespoke piece of luxury furniture, creating zero waste.

Design Process

1. Remnant Audit & Stream Mapping

Identified high-grade construction waste materials in Taipei and established structured taxonomy for material size, durability, and texture.

2. Structural Joinery Design

Developed standardized, tool-free mechanical connectors to completely replace chemical adhesives and hardware fasteners.

3. Configurator UI / UX Flow

Designed a clean interactive web prototype enabling users to easily visualize material origins, customize dimensions, and preview configurations.

4. Scalable Supply Chain Integration

Created an end-to-end framework linking local reclaimed material suppliers directly to modern CNC fabrication pipelines.
