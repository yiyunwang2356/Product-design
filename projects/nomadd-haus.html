01 / Challenge & Communication

Connecting the Physical Space with Digital Workflows

Nomadd Haus is a shared space for creativity and pop culture communities. Running the space involved managing reservations, exhibitions, and pop-up events, while keeping communication simple for users and hosts.

LINE Integration

Used LINE as the main communication channel to make booking and communication easier for local users.

Calendar Synchronization

Connected booking information with Google Calendar to help hosts manage schedules and availability.

Connecting Online and Offline

Mapped the journey between digital booking and physical space use to create a more connected experience.

Spatial Layout Exploration

Exploring Two Layout Approaches

During the planning stage, we explored two spatial layouts. Version 01 used a more linear arrangement, while Version 02 introduced angled circulation and a more flexible counter area. We combined elements from both to improve circulation and flexibility.

03 / Information Architecture

Structuring the Digital Journey

I organized the platform around the user journey, from exploring the space and choosing an event to booking and receiving an access code.

Space & Events : Explore available spaces and events. Booking : Choose a time and complete the booking. Access: Receive a temporary code to enter the space.

04 / User Interface Design

Refining the Digital Interface

The first version focused on listing rooms. I changed the approach to help users choose a space based on what they wanted to do.

THE PROBLEM

"The room-list approach made it harder for users to decide which space was right for their needs."

THE CHANGE

"I introduced filters based on activities and atmosphere, connecting user needs with different spaces."

THE SOLUTION

"The final interface helps users explore spaces based on what they want to do, then continue directly to booking."

ATMOSPHERE FILTERING

Filters help users find a space based on their activity and preferred atmosphere.
